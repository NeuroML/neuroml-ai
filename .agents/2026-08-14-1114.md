## Errors and Lessons

- Test artifacts must stay under `tmp_path`; writing to `tmp_path.parent`
  leaks files across tests sharing a run root.
- For third-party MCP servers the in-tool permission check cannot apply --
  the enforceable layers are a client-side tool policy and OS sandboxing
  (see `devdocs/mcp-permissions.md`).
