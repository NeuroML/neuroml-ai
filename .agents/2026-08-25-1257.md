## Objective

Harden `klea-stores-create` against corrupt caches and make large-corpus ingestion memory-bounded.

## Completed

- **Corrupt-cache hardening:** atomic `.pkl` writes, `EOFError`/`UnpicklingError` moved aside as `.pkl.corrupt`, pruned when healed. Live test confirmed.
- **Memory-bounded ingestion:** `chunk_all` releases per-file chunks; `store` streams cached chunks; BM25 batched pickles with EOF-loop readers (legacy single-list remains loadable).
- **Subprocess chunk workers:** uncached files convert in short-lived `spawn` workers (`--worker-mem-limit` default 4 GiB includes ~1-1.5 GiB models, `--worker-batch-size` default 200, whichever first); parent stays lean, fully-cached re-runs spawn zero workers. `build` now composes worker `chunk` + streaming `store`.
- **DOI cache:** atomic writes + batched persistence (every 25 + on close).
- **Docs:** `devdocs/system/store-create.md` (flowchart), `devdocs/adr/0001-chunk-workers.md` (MADR), `devdocs/system/mcp-permissions.md` moved and improved; `AGENTS.md` and `docs/contributing.rst` updated.

## Errors and Lessons

- `Ran out of input` was a 0-byte `.pkl` from a killed run, not OOM; `journalctl` reboot evidence was decisive. Docling leaks per conversion (docling#2788) and cannot be freed in-process — only a fresh process reclaims it.
