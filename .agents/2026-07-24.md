## Errors and Lessons

- **SIM117 is not safe for NiceGUI**  ---  Combining `with A, B, C:` contexts breaks parent-child nesting when elements at different nesting depths need sibling content after the innermost context. Must keep manual nesting with `# noqa: SIM117`.
- **`_chats` -> `chats` replaceAll**  ---  Corrupted function names `_get_chats_sorted` -> `getchats_sorted` and `_hydrate_chats` -> `hydratechats` in imports. Had to fix up manually.
- **Auto-scroll needs Quasar API, not raw DOM**  ---  `scrollTop`, `scrollIntoView`, and `requestAnimationFrame` all fail because NiceGUI batches UI+JS in the same WebSocket packet; the JS fires before the DOM is laid out. `setScrollPosition` on QScrollArea is the correct approach.
- **`ui.run_javascript` from background tasks**  ---  Raises `RuntimeError` if no client context. Use captured element references instead.
